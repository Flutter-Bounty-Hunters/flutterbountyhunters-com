CONCEPT 3, "THE CANYON". References: ref-container.png, ref-type.png.
Inside an immense, dark orbital cargo depot: a canyon of container stacks rises out of frame on both sides of a long central aisle that recedes to a distant, hazy, cold light. Awe-inspiring scale, several stories high, seen from near the floor. The left half is dominated by one gigantic container in the near foreground, its long side facing the viewer at a slight angle, filling the left half from floor to top, with "SUPER EDITOR" painted across its side in enormous, weathered, bone-white stencil letters that follow the metal panels, and the smaller line "A DOCUMENT EDITING TOOLKIT FOR FLUTTER" stenciled below. On the right side of the aisle, a stack of three containers recedes, each with its name in smaller stencil letters in correct perspective: "OVERLORD", "FOLLOW THE LEADER", "SUPER TEXT LAYOUT". Overhead gantry lights make pools of light on the wet floor between dark gaps.
Bottom fifth: the floor falls into near black, with a row of three small text entries with thin dividers: "ATTRIBUTED TEXT", "FLUTTER TEST ROBOTS", "GOLDEN BRICKS".
