CONCEPT 2, "THE ARMORY". References: ref-container.png, ref-type.png.
View straight into the open end of a huge container from just outside its doorway: perfectly symmetrical one-point perspective. Its octagonal door frame nearly fills the section, the two thick doors are swung wide at the far left and right edges. Inside, it is fitted out like a pristine special-forces armory: rows of beautifully machined hard cases in dark steel racks along both walls, lit from above by rows of thin, dim work lights that recede into the depth. Hard cases on the racks have names stenciled on their faces in worn bone-white paint, as part of the scene in correct perspective: on the left "OVERLORD", "ATTRIBUTED TEXT", "GOLDEN BRICKS"; on the right "FOLLOW THE LEADER", "SUPER TEXT LAYOUT", "TEST ROBOTS". In the center at the back, on a heavy pedestal, sits the largest, most beautiful case, under the brightest light, with "SUPER EDITOR" stenciled across it, large and fully legible.
Section title at the very top center, on darkness: "OPEN THE ARSENAL". No other text.
