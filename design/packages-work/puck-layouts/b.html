Puck
Put a menu within reach, anywhere in your Flutter app. People drag it out of the way, and it springs to the edge.
Get Puck on pub.dev
In a corner, the menu is a quarter of a ring.
At an edge, half a ring.
In the open, the whole ring, on as many levels as you need.
Try it. Drag the puck, throw it, or tap it to open its menu.
