Compare contour lines with shaded relief.

Your hikers glance at the map in bright sun, so use shaded relief. It shows how steep a trail is without more lines to read.

Contour linesShaded relief
Judging a slopeCount the linesSee it at a glance
In full sunThin lines wash outShapes stay clear
Best forPlanning at homeOut on the trail
